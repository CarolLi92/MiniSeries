**"WHEN THE PERSON YOU FOUGHT FOR LEFT"** | Runtime: ~1:50 | English-first, selected Chinese texture

## COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:12

**[BLACK. An office badge sliding across a desk. A cardboard box. The elevator doors close on someone we don't see.]**

**EFFIE (V.O.)**

When the person you fought for leaves the company, nobody tells you what comes next. You find out who else has been teaching you all along.

## THE UNTITLED MENTOR — 0:12–0:50

**[TWO-SHOT: back to back at the kitchen table. Evening. Toronto towers lit behind them.]**

**NIA**

So. After I left. Who's been teaching you?

**EFFIE**

Ken. Head of AI and data. After a launch he told me, "You're faster than anyone in this company."

**NIA**

That's a compliment.

**EFFIE**

That's what I thought. Then he said, "That may not be all a good thing. Your next step is learning how to bring people on board, so they move forward with you."

**NIA**

Ouch. And also, correct.

**EFFIE**

I'd been sprinting and calling it leadership.

**NIA**

There's a saying back home. 慢慢来，比较快. Go slower, arrive sooner. A candid mentor with no title attached? Those are rare. Keep him.

## CHOOSING THE BATTLEFIELD — 0:50–1:20

**[CUT TO: B-ROLL. A product roadmap on a whiteboard. Three women at the head of a table, then Effie walking in.]**

**NIA**

And the product side? Those women don't hand out easy wins.

**EFFIE**

They taught me to choose my battlefield. Not every meeting is a war worth fighting.

**EFFIE**

Now I tell them what I want to achieve first. Then I let them travel with me. Here's plan A. If that breaks, B. If that breaks, C.

**NIA**

So by the time you ask, they're already on the trip.

**EFFIE**

No surprises. Just a map.

## LEARNING TO BE LOCAL — 1:20–1:50

**[TWO-SHOT again. Slower. Two cups, one refilled.]**

**NIA (V.O.)**

When we first landed here, neither of us knew how to be "local." The small talk. The hockey. The Friday drinks where everyone already knew the rules.

**EFFIE**

I used to rehearse what to say at lunch.

**NIA**

I used to skip lunch.

**EFFIE (laughs)**

But every week I pushed the edge a little further. One more question in the meeting. One more "I disagree."

**NIA**

Confidence isn't handed to people who look like us here. You store it yourself, a little at a time, like savings.

**EFFIE**

Even when it's the hardest account to keep open.

## END CARD — 1:50–1:55
