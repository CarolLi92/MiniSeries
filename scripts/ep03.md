# 8090 BACK-TO-BACK — EP03 FULL SCRIPT


**"ASK FIRST, OR SORRY LATER"** | Runtime: ~1:55 | Host: Nia · Guest: Effie | English-first, selected Chinese texture

## COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:12

**[BLACK. A glass boardroom door. Through it, a long table, and one chair pushed against the wall.]**

**NIA (V.O.)**

In my last VP interview, they asked me one question. Do you ask for permission, or do it first and apologize later? I didn't get the job. So today, I'm asking someone who would have.

**[TITLE CARD:]** *Hosted by Nia.*

## THE CHAIR BY THE WALL — 0:12–0:40

**[TWO-SHOT: back to back at the kitchen table. Morning. This time Nia pours the tea.]**

**NIA**

My turn to ask the questions.

**EFFIE**

That's new.

**NIA**

Me first. My first real break, a manager let me sit in on board meetings. Not at the table. A chair by the wall. For years.

**EFFIE**

Just listening?

**NIA**

Just listening. Then one day they voted me in. Every door I walked through, someone opened it first.

**EFFIE**

And you waited for them.

**NIA**

I was very good at waiting.

## THE QUESTION I FAILED — 0:40–1:10

**NIA**

So. You. Permission, or apology?

**EFFIE**

Apology, mostly. If I'm sure the work is right, I start, and I tell people while I'm doing it.

**NIA**

And if you're wrong?

**EFFIE**

Then I'm wrong in public and I fix it in public. Early beats perfect.

**NIA**

Once I was told to cut someone from my team. I didn't. I thought her work was fine. A year later, the cut came for me.

**[BEAT. Effie doesn't rush to fix it.]**

**NIA**

I still don't know if that was kindness, or fear wearing kindness's coat.

**EFFIE**

Maybe both. Keep the kindness. Drop the waiting.

## EFFIE'S FOUR RULES — 1:10–1:35

**[B-ROLL: a phone note titled "When to move." Four lines, typed fast.]**

**NIA**

You just changed teams. Your sponsor line broke again. What's the plan?

**EFFIE**

Four rules. When someone asks whose side I'm on, answer fast. My first hard call sets my price. A deputy is leverage, not a replacement. And energy is political life, so I protect it.

**NIA**

And you're still studying for that actuarial exam. I told you to skip it and go all in on AI.

**EFFIE**

And I said no. Actuarial plus AI plus product. That's a corner nobody else is standing in.

**NIA (smiles)**

You said no to me again.

## YOUR STRATEGY — 1:35–1:55

**[TWO-SHOT. Slower. The light has moved.]**

**NIA**

Remember what I told you, when everything was falling apart? Company politics is dark.

**EFFIE**

And I said, don't worry. Your strategy is all with me.

**NIA**

And I said, 那你替我收好. Keep it safe for me.

**EFFIE (taps her temple)**

Still here. Every page.

**NIA**

Next interview, when they ask me that question, I'll tell them I've already started. I learned that from someone I used to manage.

## END CARD — 1:55–2:00

*Both of us are still learning the other way.*
