# 8090 BACK-TO-BACK — EP03 FULL SCRIPT


**"ASK FIRST, OR SORRY LATER"** | Runtime: ~1:50 | Host: Nia · Guest: Effie | English-first, selected Chinese texture

## COLD OPEN — 0:00–0:12

**[BLACK. Two cups set down on a wooden table. One slides across to the other side.]**

**NIA (V.O.)**

Twenty years in, I've learned the most useful thing a mentor can give isn't an answer. It's the right question, at the right time. Today, I'm asking her.

**[TITLE CARD:]** *Hosted by Nia.*

## WHAT I WISH I'D KNOWN — 0:12–0:35

**[TWO-SHOT: back to back at the kitchen table. Morning. This time Nia pours the tea.]**

**NIA**

My turn to ask the questions.

**EFFIE**

That's new.

**NIA**

Me first. Early in my career, I thought waiting was a form of respect. Wait to be invited. Wait to be sure.

**EFFIE**

And now?

**NIA**

Now I know waiting is also a choice. It just sends its bill later.

## PERMISSION OR APOLOGY — 0:35–1:05

**NIA**

So. You. Permission, or apology?

**EFFIE**

Apology, mostly. If I'm sure the work is right, I start.

**NIA**

Good instinct. Now the harder question. When you're wrong, who's in the room with you?

**EFFIE**

...Just me?

**NIA**

If it's just you, you're fast. If they came with you, you're leading.

**[BEAT. Effie turns her cup slowly.]**

**NIA**

Before you move, tell one person why. That's not asking permission. That's an invitation.

**EFFIE**

An invitation, not a permission.

**NIA**

先做，但别一个人做. Move first. Just don't move alone.

## STARTING OVER — 1:05–1:30

**[B-ROLL: a new badge, a new desk, an empty whiteboard.]**

**NIA**

You're on a new team. New boss, starting over. What's your first move?

**EFFIE**

Learn who really decides. Make one clear call early, so they know how I think.

**NIA**

And?

**EFFIE**

Protect my energy. It's the one thing I can't borrow back.

**NIA (smiles)**

That last one took me fifteen years. Keep going.

## YOUR STRATEGY — 1:30–1:50

**[TWO-SHOT. Slower. The light has moved.]**

**NIA**

Remember what I told you, back when we worked together?

**EFFIE**

Company politics is dark.

**NIA**

And you said...

**EFFIE**

Don't worry. Your strategy is all with me.

**NIA**

那你替我收好. Keep it safe for me.

**EFFIE (taps her temple)**

Still here. Every page.

**NIA**

Good. Now start writing your own.
